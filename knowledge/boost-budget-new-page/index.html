Most new pages lose money because they put a big budget behind one post before they know what customers like.

Step 1: Test three posts

Pick three posts with different photos or videos. Boost each one for $2 to $3 a day for three days.

Step 2: Count messages, not likes

For a seller, what matters is how many customers message you. Divide what you spent by the number of messages to get your cost per message.

Step 3: Grow the winner slowly

Turn off the posts with a high cost per message. Raise the budget on the best post by about 20% at a time, so results stay steady.

If you don't have time to do this yourself, the ADS BOX team can run your boosts and send you a report every week.
